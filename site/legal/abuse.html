NEON Torrent Abuse Reporting

Effective date: {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}}

NEON Torrent is a BitTorrent client. NEON does not host torrent files or other content, does not run trackers, and has no catalogue or search service. Content is supplied and shared by users through the BitTorrent network.

Report suspected misuse

Rights holders and other concerned people can send a report to {{ABUSE_EMAIL}}. Include enough information for us to understand the concern, such as the relevant torrent or info-hash, the material involved, the rights or safety issue, and a way to contact you. Do not send sensitive personal information that is not needed to explain the report.

What we can do

We can review information sent to us and take steps within NEON or services we operate where the issue is within our control. We may ask for clarification if a report does not identify the concern clearly. We will assess reports individually and act as required by applicable law.

What we cannot do

NEON does not possess or host the reported files. We cannot remove content from other users’ computers, control peers or trackers, or make a third-party site remove material. A report to NEON does not itself remove or disable a torrent on the wider BitTorrent network. If the content is hosted by another service, contact that service or the relevant host directly.
