First-Run & Network Setup

Windows Firewall Prompt

Windows Firewall prompts once for NeonTorrent.exe. Allow access on Private networks to enable incoming peer connections and NAT-PMP/UPnP port mapping.

Antivirus Coexistence

NEON Torrent actively reads MwacDetections every 2 seconds to absorb blocked torrent IPs into libtorrent's filter. To silence connection warnings completely, whitelist runtime\NeonTorrent.exe in your antivirus.

Disk Full Safeguard

If a destination drive fills up, transfers stop writing and transition to upload-only, displaying Disk full in red. They resume automatically when space is cleared, or use Move data to transfer files to another drive.

Instant Background Shutdown

Quitting hides the UI window immediately. Resume data, fastresume states, and network caches are safely persisted to disk in the background within seconds, eliminating annoying UI freezing.
